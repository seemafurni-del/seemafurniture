Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Seema Furniture and Aluminium** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit our website, located at https://seemafurniture.netlify.app (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Directly Provide

When you contact us, make an inquiry, or chat with us via the floating WhatsApp button, you may provide us with the following personal information:

  • **Contact Data:** Your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share in your messages or inquiries.
  • **Location Data:** Your location (Jaipur/Rajasthan) if necessary for a furniture delivery or consultation.

B. Information Collected Automatically (Cookies and Tracking)

When you access the Site, certain information is automatically collected through third-party services:

  • **Usage Data:** Information about how you use and interact with the Site, such as the pages you view, the time spent on those pages, and the sequence of pages visited.
  • **Device Data:** Your device’s Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, and mobile network information.
  • **Advertising Identifiers:** Information collected by **Google AdSense** and related advertising partners through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • **To Provide Services:** To respond to your inquiries, fulfill custom furniture requests, and provide you with design consultations.
  • **To Communicate:** To send you updates, promotional materials, and relevant information about our products (only if you opt-in or inquire).
  • **For Advertising and Analytics (Google AdSense):** Our site uses **Google AdSense** to serve advertisements. AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to measure the effectiveness of the ads and to show you relevant ads based on your visit to this and other websites (this is known as **interest-based advertising**).
  • **To Improve the Site:** To understand which products and sections are most popular so we can enhance our website and services.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others. We only share your information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • **Service Providers:** We share necessary information with service providers like our website host and the **Google AdSense platform** to operate our business and deliver advertising.
  • **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court order).

4. Third-Party Services and Cookies

A. Google AdSense

By using our Site, you acknowledge and agree that **Google AdSense** will collect and use data for personalized and non-personalized advertising. You can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

B. WhatsApp

When you click on the floating WhatsApp icon, you are connecting directly to our business account. Your usage of WhatsApp is governed by **WhatsApp's privacy policy** and terms of service. We only receive the information you choose to share in that conversation.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • **Opt-Out of Interest-Based Ads:** You can opt-out of Google’s personalized ads using the link provided above.
  • **Disable Cookies:** You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some portions of our Site may not function properly.
